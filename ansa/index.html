Website review: four quick wins

I walked through ansabank.com as a customer on 20 November 2026. Each item below is small to fix and affects whether a visitor becomes a lead.

1

Personal loans say "Calculate Now" where other loans say "Apply Now"

The vehicle loan and mortgage pages lead with "Apply Now". The personal loans page sends visitors into the same calculator and pre-approval flow with a "Calculate Now" button, which undersells it to someone ready to borrow.

Fix: use the same "Apply Now" wording on every loan page, and test both labels to see which brings more applications.

2

The car loan calculator labels total repayments as "total cost of credit"

With the default settings (TTD 100,000 vehicle, TTD 5,000 down, 7 years at 5.00% APR) the calculator shows TTD 1,342.72 a month and a "total cost of credit" of TTD 112,788.59. That figure is the total of all repayments. The interest cost is about TTD 17,789.

Fix: show "Total repayments" and "Interest cost" as two lines. A smaller, clearly explained cost of credit is also a stronger selling point.

3

The footer FAQ link points to a staging site

The FAQ link in the site footer goes to staging.ansabank.com, a test environment, while the main menu points to the live FAQ page.

Fix: point the footer link to ansabank.com/faqs and add a regular broken-link check to the monthly report.

4

Pre-approval asks for income before showing value

"Check Eligibility" moves straight to employment status, household income and existing repayments. That is the right information for the bank, asked at a point where many visitors are still browsing.

Fix: test a lighter first step (product, amount, phone or WhatsApp) and collect income details in the follow-up. I would A/B test both versions and keep the one with more approved loans.

This review covers public pages only. I have not seen ANSA Bank's analytics, lead data or internal processes, and my first month would start by measuring all of the above properly.
